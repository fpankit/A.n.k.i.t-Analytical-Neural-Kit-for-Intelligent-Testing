Security Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

1. Our Approach

Security is a foundational part of everything we build. We design our systems, automations, and websites with security controls built in from the start, not added on after the fact.

2. Data Protection Measures

We protect data using encryption in transit and at rest, strict role-based access controls, secure development practices, and continuous monitoring of our infrastructure for anomalies.

3. Incident Response

In the event of a security incident, we follow a documented response plan to contain the impact, investigate thoroughly, and notify affected parties and relevant authorities as required.

4. Your Responsibilities

We encourage our clients to use strong, unique passwords and enable two-factor authentication wherever possible. Report any suspected vulnerability or breach to contact@21st.dev.
