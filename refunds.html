Refund Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

1. General Policy

Our services are delivered as custom projects under individual agreements. Because work is tailored to each client, refund eligibility is determined on a per-project basis as described in your agreement.

2. Refund Eligibility

You may be eligible for a refund if we fail to deliver the agreed scope of work, or if a project is cancelled before substantial work has begun. No refunds are issued for completed or in-progress deliverables.

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, email contact@21st.dev with your project details and the reason for the request. We will review and respond within 5 business days.

4. Exceptions

Subscription-style services, licenses, and work already delivered or in final review stages are generally non-refundable unless otherwise stated in your agreement.
