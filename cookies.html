Last updated: August 6, 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and understand how visitors use the site.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use essential cookies required for the site to function, and analytics cookies that help us understand page usage and improve our content. We do not use cookies for advertising.

3. Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling certain cookies may affect how the website functions.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some third-party services we use (such as analytics providers and the 3D content platform) may set their own cookies. Their use is governed by their respective privacy policies.
