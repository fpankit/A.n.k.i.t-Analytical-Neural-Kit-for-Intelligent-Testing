# Brand Design & Color Palette Guideline

This document contains the brand design system, color codes, typography settings, and visual layout specifications extracted from the cyber-futurist AI-Design Agency mockup.

---

## 1. Core Visual Concept
* **Theme**: Cyber-futurism, artificial intelligence, cosmic depth, and high-end creative agency.
* **Aesthetic Paradigm**: Sleek dark mode, **Glassmorphism** (translucent panels with high background blur), **3D organic/liquid obsidian elements**, and intense **luminous neon glow** accents.
* **Tone**: Premium, cutting-edge, professional, immersive, and sleek.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Lighting (Hex & HSL Specs)

### A. Primary Background & Canvas
* **Obsidian Void (Main Background)**: `#020205` / `hsl(240, 43%, 1%)`
  * *Role*: Deep space dark canvas with a subtle blue/violet tint.
* **Space Nebula (Radial Backlight Glow)**: `#002244` / `hsl(210, 100%, 13%)`
  * *Role*: Soft, wide radial gradient positioned behind the central emblem for a backlit glow.
* **Glassmorphic Fill (Containers)**: `rgba(10, 15, 30, 0.4)`
  * *Role*: Semi-transparent dark blue-grey card backgrounds.

### B. Accent & Luminous Glows
* **Electric Cyan (Primary Accent / Glowing Logo)**: `#00a2ff` to `#00e5ff` / `hsl(202, 100%, 50%)` to `hsl(187, 100%, 50%)`
  * *Role*: Logo reflections, active navigation links, border outlines, and gradient text highlights.
* **Luminous Flare (Core Hotspot)**: `#ffffff` / `hsl(0, 0%, 100%)`
  * *Role*: Central starburst flare overlays.

### C. Typography & UI Text
* **Hyper-White (Primary Text)**: `#ffffff` / `hsl(0, 0%, 100%)`
  * *Role*: Main headers and high-contrast numbers.
* **Muted Cool Grey (Secondary/Body Text)**: `#8a99ad` / `hsl(214, 20%, 61%)`
  * *Role*: Minor labels, pagination, descriptions, and passive links.

---

## 3. Typography System

* **Primary Headings**:
  * *Font*: Clean, ultra-geometric sans-serif (e.g., **Inter**, **SF Pro Display**, or **Outfit**).
  * *Weight*: Black/Extra Bold (`800` or `900`).
  * *Spacing*: Tight letter-spacing (`letter-spacing: -0.04em`) and tight line-height.
  * *Effects*: Linear text gradients from hyper-white to electric cyan (applied to highlights like "Design Team").
* **Secondary Labels & System UI**:
  * *Font*: Geometric sans-serif or sub-mono (e.g., **Space Mono** or **Plus Jakarta Sans**).
  * *Weight*: Medium (`500`).
  * *Style*: Small font sizes (`11px` to `14px`) with wide letter-spacing (`0.1em`).

---

## 4. Key Visual & Layout Elements

### A. Central 3D Emblem (Logo)
* **Form**: A circular pill-like shield split into four symmetrical quadrants.
* **Material**: Semi-transparent cyan glass/sapphire.
* **Effects**: Strong inner glow, reflective surface highlights, and a bright white central starburst flare.

### B. Specular 3D Hands (Reaching Elements)
* **Form**: Two hyper-realistic humanoid hands. Left hand reaches up from the bottom-left; right hand reaches down from the top-right.
* **Material**: Liquid obsidian or glossy black chrome.
* **Reflective Properties**: Highly specular, catching cyan neon reflections on the edges and white highlight reflections on the contours.

### C. Glassmorphic UI Components (Cards)
* **Border**: 1px solid border at low opacity (`rgba(0, 162, 255, 0.15)`).
* **Blur**: `backdrop-filter: blur(30px) saturate(150%)`.
* **Corners**: Highly rounded (`border-radius: 24px` to `32px`).
* **Layout Blocks**:
  * **Bottom-Left**: Stats card with `3M+ Happy people` and overlapping avatar icons.
  * **Right Stack**: Secondary stat cards (`100+ Projects`, `250+ Trusted Partners`).
  * **Bottom Center**: Social presence links formatted as small, glassmorphic squares with rounded corners.
